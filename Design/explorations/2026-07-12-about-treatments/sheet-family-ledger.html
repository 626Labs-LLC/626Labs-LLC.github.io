Q7

626, i think this part of the number is called the exchange, in northside buildings didn't get painted much so their old phone numbers stood for years 626 as I passed by, leaving the neighborhood each day for school. I know people "rep" their area code, but this isn't that, 626 is the shape of homebase. The dirt and the asphalt that I played on, the place I always wanted to go back to when I was far. Where I was protected, challenged, and built. Like I said before I never got much from school, as soon as kids became more aware the differences that a kid can't change started to matter more that number got used a lot more. But not for incoming calls, for data. I had an outlet when I didn't fit in and it wasn't a shady group, but technology itself. How can I make this computer faster, how do i fix it now, how can I make this mine.

I could always use Stage Productions. I love a good film, I am not a disney freak, but when I was younger I really saw myself in Stitch and when it was revealed he was project 626 I felt like we were intertwined.
