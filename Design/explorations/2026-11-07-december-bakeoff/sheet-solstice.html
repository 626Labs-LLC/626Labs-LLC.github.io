02 · The founding

626Labs builds exact-fit software.

I build tools, because care doesn't always scale.

626Labs builds exact-fit software. The founding argument is simple: it has never been easier to have the exact tools we need, and too much money has been going to too many companies for tools that only half fit. So we build and maintain our own, at a quality that rivals enterprise software, made to enhance the work people do instead of taking their jobs. We are here to make great products, even if only a handful of people need them. That's ok.
