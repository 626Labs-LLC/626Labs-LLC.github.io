# RoRoRo grew eyes, hands, and a heartbeat

*Two months ago RoRoRo was a Roblox multi-launcher you could recommend on camera. This week it became a platform: version 1.8 on the Microsoft Store, and three plugins that watch, act, and keep your alts alive. All local, all consent-gated.*

Two months ago I shipped RoRoRo with one promise: a Roblox multi-launcher you could hand a clan without a disclaimer. Packaged, signed, honest about what it does. That was a launcher. This week it grew into a platform. RoRoRo 1.8 is on the Microsoft Store, and it now has three plugins that turn a wall of idle alts into something that watches itself, does the work you recorded, and refuses to time out. Eyes, hands, and a heartbeat.

The thing that makes them a family instead of three unrelated tools is one rule I carried over from the launcher: every capability a plugin uses is disclosed, opt-in, and local. A plugin tells you exactly what it wants to do, RoRoRo shows you that list on a consent sheet before a line of it runs, and nothing touches the network it wasn't already touching. That rule is the spine. Everything else hangs off it.

## Hands

**Ur Task** is the record-and-replay macro plugin, and it's the hands. Record a farming sequence once, play it on any alt, or a whole batch of them in order. The macros are portable now, so one recording runs across your entire roster. And it's window-aware: tile your Roblox windows however you like and the clicks still land where they should, because playback maps every event onto the target window wherever it sits.

## Eyes

**Ur OCR** is perception. Point it at a region of your screen, tell it what to watch for (a piece of text, a color), and when it matches, it fires a keybind. On its own that's a useful reflex. Wired to Ur Task, it fires a whole macro instead of a single key. A banner appears, the eyes see it, and the right sequence presses itself. Perception to action, and neither half leaves your PC.

That last part is the whole reason this is a family and not a bundle. Ur OCR talks to Ur Task over a local pipe on your own machine, current-user only, gated on both ends. The eyes tell the hands what to do, natively, with no cloud round-trip. Each plugin multiplies the others instead of just sitting next to them.

## Heartbeat

**Ur AFK** is the simple one, on purpose. Roblox kicks an idle client after about twenty minutes, and if a stack of your alts idles out together they all reconnect together, which is exactly what trips the captcha wall. Ur AFK watches how long each account has gone untouched and, right before the timeout, taps a key to keep it alive, then hands your focus back. One key, ever, and you always see it coming. It's the plugin for the person installing their first one and reading their first consent sheet. It's also a good backup for the nights you forget to start a macro.

## A ladder, not a pile

The three sort into a ladder. Ur AFK is the bottom rung: one toggle, one key, nothing to learn. Ur Task is the middle: record once, play anywhere. Ur OCR driving Ur Task is the top: build a perception-to-action loop and let it run. You start at the bottom and climb only as far as you want, and the same consent sheet, the same visible countdowns, the same abort keys, and the same permanent no on ever touching a captcha hold at every step.

The launcher you could recommend on camera became a platform you can still recommend on camera. That was the only spec that mattered.
