The first five minutes are where a new user decides your app is for them. Walk them to the moment it clicks, and they stay.

That's what a good onboarding tour is for — and it's the kind of high-leverage polish that's well worth a Saturday afternoon.

vibe-walk is a new Claude Code plugin that builds one. Point it at your app and it:

- reads your user-facing surfaces and names the aha moment — the first time your product actually clicks
- builds a fully-instrumented spotlight tour you own outright: a Driver.js module, an anchor-injection codemod, and six analytics events
- measures the right thing — downstream activation, not a "tour completed" badge that means nothing

Same ownership model as shadcn: the generated code is yours from day one. No runtime SDK, no vendor, no lock-in.

It's honest about fit, too: if a guided tour genuinely won't help your app, it tells you before you build one. But for most first runs, a short, well-placed tour is exactly what's missing.

vibe-walk is the tenth plugin in the Vibe Plugins family for Claude Code. Web, MIT, free.

Start the weekend with a little vibe-walk: https://626labs.dev/vibe-walk/
