# LinkedIn draft — games section launch (2026-09-03)

Shipped a small arcade onto the 626 Labs homepage today.

Two new movie games to add to the Bacon Trail, fully embedded — no logins, no backends, instant play:

- Box Office Heads Up: two posters, one question — which opened bigger? 137 films, 7,604 possible matchups, streaks multiply your score.
- Tag That Line: a classic tagline, four posters, tap the right one. 141 lines spanning 1972–2024, curated classics plus a weekly fresh sweep.
- Birthday Bacon Trail: walk a film chain from today's birthday actors to Kevin Bacon. New cast every day.

The builder note: the two new games started life inside a different app of mine. This afternoon they were captured as portable feature bundles and now they are planted as standalone widgets sharing one 137-film dataset — opening-weekend numbers powering one game, taglines the other. Same data, two games, zero servers.

Play them here: 626labs.dev/#play
