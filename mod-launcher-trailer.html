What the trailer shows

626 Mod Launcher is a free mod manager for Windows, built on one rule: your files are yours.

  • Off means moved aside, never deleted. Flip a mod back on and it's where it was.
  • Steam games found automatically, and a signed game feed adds new games without an app update.
  • Save snapshots before you experiment, one click to go back.
  • Back up everything: every game's mods, saves and settings in one file, and restore exactly what you choose.
  • No ads, no telemetry, no account.
